# Ingenia archetypes (v2, 2026-10-03)

Eight archetypes, seven houses: one sits out each seed at random, so "who's missing" is part of the seed's novelty. Archetypes are weight tables over one shared tool catalog, not separate code. "Favorite / least favorite" are starting leanings only; seeds bend them. Every pair must have a one-sentence friction or affinity.

Herald was removed (too close to the Socialite); Normie added as the baseline; Faithful added as the inherited-worldview case.

| Archetype | Engine                               | Information role                                | Observer stance (starting)                                                                                 | Wound (discoverable)                                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Normie    | baseline; ordinary decent life       | passes things along, mostly accurately          | last to form any belief — too busy                                                                         | tired; a job and a family off-street                   |
| Skeptic   | debunking                            | verifier; lowers confidence of facts he touches | "it's the furnace"                                                                                         | being right has cost him people                        |
| Mystic    | superstition and ritual              | amplifier of anything strange                   | first and fastest believer; leaves offerings                                                               | needs the universe to mean something                   |
| Faithful  | meaning-making; shows up for people  | reframer; facts become parables                 | slowest and most stable; the Observer must fit her theology or be rejected; a shift is a theological event | faith is holding something together                    |
| Trickster | chaos; pranks                        | deliberate planter of false facts               | tests it relentlessly                                                                                      | wants to be caught                                     |
| Recluse   | mystery                              | hoarder                                         | slowest to believe; most likely to end up writing to the player                                            | TBD per seed                                           |
| Warden    | conflict; grievances with long fuses | judge; facts → grievances                       | authority to appeal to, or threat to order                                                                 | the street is the only thing she still controls        |
| Socialite | moves bodies; multi-party events     | connector (absorbs the Herald's relay role)     | wants everyone to agree on it together                                                                     | lonely; the parties are how she doesn't think about it |

## Starting leanings

- **Normie**: likes everyone a little; closest to the Faithful (church sometimes) and the Socialite; avoids the Warden's notes. The player's proxy and the belief experiment's control group.
- **Skeptic**: favorite Recluse (doesn't perform); respectful opponent of the Faithful (two complete systems); least favorite Mystic.
- **Mystic**: favorite Recluse (doesn't argue); least favorite Skeptic; sharpest conflict is actually the Faithful — same turf, doctrine vs. vibes; uneasy allies against the Skeptic in some seeds.
- **Faithful**: project = the Normie; respectful opponent = Skeptic; conflict = Mystic; looks allied with the Warden but splits over grace vs. rules; cannot be provoked by the Trickster (brings him a pie); may be the one person the Recluse writes back to; potluck rivalry with the Socialite.
- **Trickster**: favorites Mystic (believes anything) and Socialite (crowds); least favorite Warden — though in some seeds they are the only two who understand each other; escalates against the Faithful's calm.
- **Recluse**: nobody at first; drifts toward whoever leaves him alone correctly (Mystic, Skeptic, Faithful); least favorite Socialite, whose kindness is a siege.
- **Warden**: favorite Skeptic (standards); least favorite Trickster and the Recluse's yard; the Faithful's forgiveness of the Trickster is what ends their alliance.
- **Socialite**: favorite everyone, loudly; perpetual siege on the Recluse; rival hostess to the Faithful.

## Tone rules

- The joke is never that the Faithful believes; it is how the others react to someone at peace. Keep denomination vague (1985 Midwest, "church," a casserole, a Sunday hat). She is the most competent person on the street at what she does.
- Eccentricity only reads against normal; the Normie exists so the other six are funny.
- Wounds are a second layer the player discovers, not the first thing the engine shows.
